# GVS v13M — Hollow Surface Intelligent Region

**Status:** architecture/model pass; physical multi-octet closure open. v13M does not replace the accepted v13A computational primitives or the v13K neurovascular anatomy. It composes them into a larger intelligent surface region and adds routing/cost rules learned from v13L/v13M experiments.

## Core idea
The hollow chip is used to make **local intelligent regions cheaper to place, connect, cool and service**, not merely to create a visually three-dimensional chip.

Cells may occupy outer, inner, underside and side surfaces where manufacturing permits. Large exact/memory/power/recovery/thermal components may occupy inside or outside Component Bays according to access and heat cost.

Each ordinary cell/cluster retains three mandatory physical service roles and one optional role:
- **Nerve:** low-swing firing/event path;
- **Charge Artery:** low-voltage collection path for charge that has finished its information role;
- **Thermal Capillary:** passive heat path toward a shared thermal artery/exhaust;
- **Light Nerve:** optional optical path only for long/reused routes that pass lifetime break-even.

No per-cell microcontroller, ADC, recovery scheduler or calibration machine is introduced.

## Intelligence organization — Hollow Surface Intelligent Octet
Eight heterogeneous Glyph cells share one existing Regional Event Lease:
- two Grammar cells;
- two static-template cells;
- two short Passive-Myelin relation cells;
- one competition/context/regeneration cell;
- one robust boundary/exact-handoff cell.

The exact role mix is compiler/workload dependent; the eight-cell model is the first concrete composition, not a fixed neural taxonomy.

### Why the octet matters
The main energy advantage is not the shape of the shell. It is the ability to do several related local operations after one long validated selection.

v13M0 preserved-cost model: about **77.8% lower selection + local-core proxy** than eight separate long selections for the tested mixed octet workloads.

## Physical adjacency rule
1. touching paired cells -> direct fourth-face connection;
2. nearby/non-neighbor -> short Nerve/subwire;
3. regional fanout -> Autonomic Event Spine;
4. stable medium relation -> promoted electrical/Myelin chord;
5. long/hot/reused -> optional Light Nerve if lifetime energy wins;
6. uncertain/cold/changing -> exact/global fabric.

## Surface routing rule
The surfaces are a **service and placement fabric**, not a mandatory signal manifold.

v13M1 found that forcing every boundary-to-boundary signal to remain on a 10 x 10 x 2 mm shell has a small mean penalty but a severe tail: directly opposite cells can pay a 5x path-length penalty. Therefore short protected interior bridges/chords remain legal when they materially reduce total cost.

## v13L isolation rule retained
Near weak differential Grammar evidence:
- avoid long one-sided parallel Nerve/Artery routing;
- prefer orthogonal crossings;
- use shielding when extracted differential service coupling or density requires it;
- sign off GC and GR coupling separately;
- do not compensate for bad geometry with new control logic.

## Hollow area/cost rule
For the preserved illustrative 10 x 10 x 2 mm shell:
- inner+outer area = 525.76 mm^2;
- outer-only = 280 mm^2.

Usable area remains above outer-only while service/support reservation is below about **46.74%**. At 20/30/40% reserve the geometry yields about 1.50x/1.31x/1.13x outer-only usable area. At 50% reserve it loses the advantage.

Therefore service anatomy must be narrow/shared. Do not consume the recovered surface with duplicated controllers or oversized per-cell utilities.

## Optical rule
Using the preserved v13I lifetime proxy:
- short 1-2 mm relations remain electrical in the tested range;
- around 5 mm optical first becomes economic only at high reuse (~128 tested uses);
- 10 mm ~64 uses;
- 15 mm ~32 uses;
- 20 mm ~16 uses.

Optics is installed/promoted only where source idle power, alignment/manufacturing cost and reuse still permit a net win.

## Recovery rule — intentionally simple
A cell's charge performs its lease/context/familiarity/evidence role first. When that state is normally finished, a one-way recovery path may move remaining charge into the regional Charge Artery/reservoir.

Recovery failure may reduce recovered energy but may not change the robust computation result. There is no per-cell recovery computer.

## Thermal rule
Heat follows passive Thermal Capillaries to shared larger thermal structures. Cooling is primary; harvesting electricity from heat is secondary and cannot justify an inefficient compute design.

## Exact/microcontroller-like hardware
If exact control is required, it lives in shared **Exact Service Cores** / Component Bays rather than in every cell. Frequently accessed blocks tend toward central/interior placement; hot/large/test/power blocks tend toward exterior/backside placement when the route cost permits.
