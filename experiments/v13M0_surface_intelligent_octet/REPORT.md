# Neural Glyph v13M0 — Hollow Surface Intelligent Octet

**Verdict: MODEL PASS / PHYSICAL COMPOSITION OPEN.** The first v13M system composition should be an eight-cell mixed intelligent region behind one existing Regional Event Lease. The hollow/all-surface organization is useful mainly because it increases usable placement surface and gives services separate physical anatomy; it is not the source of intelligence by itself. The intelligence gain comes from letting Grammar/template/Myelin/context/competition cooperate locally without paying a new long selection for every small operation.

## Why v13M exists
v13K established the neurovascular organization: direct fourth-face neighbors, low-swing Nerve, low-voltage Charge Artery, passive Thermal Capillary, optional optical Light Nerve and inside/outside Component Bays. v13L showed that those electrical services can be routed near a weak differential Grammar pair when geometry makes coupling common-mode rather than one-sided.

The next question is therefore system-level: does the hollow surface arrangement help a real mixed local region enough to justify its physical complexity?

## New term
**Hollow Surface Intelligent Octet (HSIO):** eight heterogeneous Glyph cells sharing one Regional Event Lease, placed on a compact hollow/surface neighborhood so frequent relations can use direct fourth-face links while Nerve/Artery/Thermal services occupy separate protected routes.

This is a composition of existing hardware, not a new transistor primitive.

## Region used in the model
Eight existing primitive roles:
- two Grammar cells (`G0`, `G1`);
- two static-template cells (`T0`, `T1`);
- two short Passive-Myelin relation cells (`M0`, `M1`);
- one local competition/context/regeneration cell (`C`);
- one robust boundary/exact-handoff cell (`B`).

The region deliberately follows the earlier v13B rule: Passive Myelin is short; weak evidence is regenerated before deep analog-chain collapse.

## Preserved energy proxies
No new physical-energy measurement is claimed. The model reuses repository values:
- long coordinate selection: 680 fJ;
- eight-way lease activity: 106.8 fJ;
- conservative physical Grammar/checkpoint readout: 134 fJ;
- static template event: 0.644 fJ;
- Passive-Myelin extra source work: 0.0132 fJ;
- direct fourth-face neighboring event: 0.15 fJ;
- regional event-spine selected tap: 0.67 fJ.

## Placement comparison
The same eight primitive roles were optimally mapped onto:
1. a flat 2 x 4 local arrangement;
2. a 2 x 2 x 2 surface/corner arrangement representing a compact all-surface neighborhood.

Four workload graphs were tested. The placement search exhaustively checks all primitive assignments (with one symmetry fixed), so the comparison is not biased by a hand-picked mapping.

### Result
| workload | flat comm | surface comm | surface communication change | shared-region total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| motif-local | 5.16 fJ | 5.16 fJ | 0.0% | 1329.27 fJ |
| context/fanout | 6.72 fJ | 5.68 fJ | **15.48% lower** | 1329.79 fJ |
| mixed reasoning | 5.23 fJ | 4.71 fJ | **9.94% lower** | 1328.82 fJ |
| cross-region stress | 5.98 fJ | 5.98 fJ | 0.0% | 1330.09 fJ |

The geometry can reduce local communication for some interaction graphs because a compact three-dimensional neighborhood supplies more useful adjacency choices, but it does not win every graph. That is important: **do not justify hollow construction by claiming every local wire becomes shorter.**

## Regional-Lease result
Across all four workloads, the shared eight-cell region is about **77.8% lower in this selection + local-core proxy** than giving each of the eight primitives its own 680 fJ long selection.

This is the dominant system effect. The few-fJ direct/spine placement improvement is real but small compared with avoiding repeated long selection.

Therefore:
- local intelligence comes from heterogeneous primitive cooperation behind one lease;
- hollow placement helps fit and connect more such regions and keeps service anatomy separated;
- hollow geometry is an enabler, not a substitute for the local computation architecture.

## All-surface capacity screen
Using the preserved v13K 10 x 10 x 2 mm shell geometry:
- outer-only surface: 280 mm^2;
- inner + outer surface: 525.76 mm^2.

After reserving surface for Nerve/Artery/Thermal/optical/support:
- 20% service reserve -> **1.50x** outer-only usable area;
- 30% -> **1.31x**;
- 40% -> **1.13x**;
- 50% -> **0.94x**, so the geometry advantage is lost.

The break-even service reservation is about **46.74%**. If services/support consume more than that, this specific shell no longer provides an active-area advantage over the outer surface alone.

This is an important v13M cost rule: the hollow interior is not free. Keep service anatomy narrow/shared enough that it does not consume the area it was meant to unlock.

## Optional optical rule rechecked
The preserved electrical and optical lifetime proxies were rescreened:
- 1-2 mm: optical never wins in the tested 8..256-use range;
- 5 mm: first tested win at about 128 uses;
- 10 mm: about 64 uses;
- 15 mm: about 32 uses;
- 20 mm: about 16 uses.

So v13M keeps optical Light Nerves only for long, repeatedly used relations. No optical equipment is added to every cell.

## What this solved
The hollow architecture now has a clearer reason to exist:
1. increase usable placement surface when service reservation stays below break-even;
2. allow computation skin and service anatomy to be spatially separated;
3. provide more placement choices for frequently interacting primitives;
4. keep hot/large/exact components in inside or outside Component Bays according to communication/thermal cost;
5. permit selected long optical routes without making optics universal.

It does **not** prove that hollow packaging is cheaper to manufacture today. Literal active inner-wall fabrication remains FUTURE_PROCESS.

## Problem still open
The v13M octet is a system model using preserved measured block costs. The eight physical primitives have not yet been co-laid out as one complete region with Nerve + Charge Artery + Thermal attachment.

## Next
Run v13M1 to test the dangerous alternative assumption: forcing communication to stay on the hollow surface. If surface-only routes create large detours, v13M must retain direct/internal promoted chords rather than turning the shell into a mandatory routing path.
