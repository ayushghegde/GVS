# v14U Idea Ledger

This file prevents useful project ideas from silently disappearing between versions.

## Retained
- **Electric polarity:** HZO ferroelectric polarization remains the durable learned-route state.
- **Guided-gap firing:** volatile guided diffusive gap remains the low-voltage inference event mechanism.
- **Field isolation:** HZO and Ag/diffusive material are physically separated; coupling is electric-field only.
- **Electricity harvesting:** regional charge recovery on program rails where recovered energy exceeds control cost; no per-cell generator.
- **Hollow chip / inside + outside:** top surface carries semantic cells; inner dry ribbed cavity surfaces carry shared charge reservoirs; backside/package may later carry separate cooling.
- **Repair branches:** local spare branches remain preferable to duplicating whole cells.
- **Usage eligibility:** recent volatile gap residue may be a temporary eligibility trace; no standalone eligibility-memory device.
- **Passive addressing:** dynamic precharge/evaluate plus fixed directional two-terminal mismatch devices.
- **Sparse active gain:** active voltage restoration is shared regionally instead of repeated in cells.

## Rejected
- literal magnetic/electromagnetic branch programming;
- per-cell energy harvester;
- liquid coolant touching HZO/Ag/reservoir electrical surfaces;
- one huge unsupported hollow cavity;
- always-biased reservoir rails;
- per-branch MOS selector;
- repeated 40-MOS Program Porch every eight cells;
- pretending a passive network can create power gain;
- 100-ns write pulse under the present inhibit-variation model.

## Still open
- fabricated 4-nm HZO FIC switching;
- fabricated passive directional mismatch device;
- full regional active-spine layout/PEX;
- cavity process/yield statistics;
- full process-stack lifetime.
