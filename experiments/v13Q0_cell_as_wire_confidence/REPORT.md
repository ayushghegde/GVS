# Neural Glyph v13Q0 — Cell-as-Wire + Population Confidence

**Verdict: MODEL PASS / PHYSICAL 3-D CONTACT CLOSURE OPEN.** v13Q0 supports replacing many dedicated local signal wires with selectively conducting cell-to-cell contacts, while representing uncertainty as population disagreement rather than forcing a single unsure cell to guess.

## New inventions

### Embodied Conduction Cell (ECC)
A Glyph cell whose boundary is simultaneously computation surface, local memory interface and selectable communication interface. The cell does not need a separate routed wire to every local neighbour; an event can be regenerated by the next cell and continue through a chain of cells.

This does **not** mean metal disappears. Each cell still needs short internal conductors and contact structures. The invention removes a separate long local wire channel where the packed cell fabric itself can carry the event.

### Contact Aperture
A normally non-conducting selectable contact between neighbouring ECCs. In a cubical packing a cell may have up to 6 face-neighbour, 12 edge-neighbour and 8 vertex-neighbour candidate contacts. Only contacts participating in the current local relation/event are opened.

Face contacts are the cheapest/default. Edge and vertex contacts are optional diagonal shortcuts and are retained only if extracted capacitance/area/energy justify them.

### Population Confidence Field (PCF)
A candidate answer is represented by activity in a group of cells rather than one cell. Confidence is the physical separation between the leading population and competing populations. Low separation means the network continues accumulating/recurrent processing instead of producing a robust event.

### Quorum Relay
A relay cell regenerates an event only when several selected neighbouring contacts agree strongly enough. Weak/disagreeing contact activity leaves the cell quiet and allows more local evidence/retry instead of propagating a possibly wrong event.

## Why v13Q changes the architecture
v13P showed that dynamic binding can be robust but hard constraint reasoning still degrades with scale. The remaining failure mode is **unsureness**: a single local state can be forced to choose before enough evidence exists.

Biological neural circuits suggest a different mechanism: information and uncertainty can be distributed across populations; recurrent excitation can accumulate evidence; inhibitory competition separates alternatives; local dendritic compartments perform nonlinear input integration; and direct electrical coupling between contacting neurons is physically possible.

v13Q translates those principles into GVS using ordinary local state, competition and selectable contacts rather than adding an exact computer.

## Experiment A — Cells as local wires
Model: 100,000 random source/destination pairs in a 16 x 16 x 4 packed-cell lattice.

Compare:
- face-only movement: six-neighbour Manhattan routing;
- embodied movement: face + edge + vertex candidate contacts, using the minimum number of cell relays.

The direct-face event proxy remains the preserved 0.15 fJ/event. Edge/vertex contacts are not physically measured, so their relative costs are swept.

### Result
Mean face-only relay count: **11.877 hops**.

Mean embodied relay count: **7.476 hops**.

Relay-count reduction: **37.05%**.

If edge contacts cost 1.5x a face contact and vertex contacts 2x:
- face-only proxy: ~1.782 fJ/path;
- embodied proxy: ~1.451 fJ/path;
- reduction: **18.53%**.

If edge/vertex contacts rise to 2x/3x, the energy advantage disappears even though hop count remains lower.

**Decision:** keep diagonal contacts as selective shortcuts only. Do not make every edge/vertex electrically heavy merely because geometry allows it.

## Experiment B — Unsureness as population disagreement
Four candidate populations, sixteen cells per population. Every local step adds noisy evidence. A robust decision is emitted only when winner minus runner-up accumulated evidence reaches a fixed confidence margin.

At progressively stronger evidence:
- drift 0.08: 67.085% correct robust, 1.915% wrong robust, 31.0% still uncertain;
- drift 0.12: 92.58% correct, 0.255% wrong, 7.165% still uncertain;
- drift 0.18: **99.8% correct, 0.01% wrong, 0.19% still uncertain**;
- drift 0.25: **100% correct, 0 wrong, 0 unresolved** in 20,000 trials.

The important behavior is not merely higher accuracy. The circuit has a physical representation for uncertainty: **small population margin = do not commit yet**.

Hard cases can therefore spend more local settling time without invoking a processor.

## Experiment C — Quorum relay under noisy contacts
Stress: sixteen relay hops; each selected contact independently has a 10% chance of presenting the wrong binary event during one local attempt.

A single-contact chain loses reliability rapidly. With local redundancy and a margin requirement:
- 5 supporting contacts: ~0.785% wrong end-to-end, ~0.05% stalled;
- 7 supporting contacts: ~0.325% wrong, no stalls in 20,000 trials;
- 9 supporting contacts: ~0.085% wrong, no stalls, only ~1.008 local attempts per planned hop on average.

This is not final fault-tolerance signoff. It proves that the packed cell fabric can trade a small amount of local population redundancy for much stronger event integrity without a central error-correction processor.

## What happened
The user's 'make the cells the wire' idea is technically useful when interpreted as **regenerative local conduction through computing cells**, not as literally eliminating all conductor material.

The biggest benefit is architectural: local routing, confidence filtering and computation can occupy the same repeated cell fabric instead of separate router + wire + compute blocks.

## Problem found
A cell with all 26 contacts permanently active would be a capacitance/crosstalk disaster and would waste area. v13Q therefore requires candidate contacts to be mostly closed and locally opened only for an active relation.

The population-confidence model also shows that very weak evidence should remain unresolved; forcing a decision would recreate the original unsureness problem.

## Physical implementation direction
### Near-term planar/stacked implementation
- repeated cells on conventional dies/stacked layers;
- face-like local contacts through ordinary short metal/inter-tier bonds;
- selected diagonal contacts represented by short local corner/edge interconnect where worthwhile;
- outer protective/package surface may carry an additional cell/service layer, but ordinary passivation itself is not assumed to contain active transistors;
- Nerve becomes a dedicated structure only when a direct regenerative cell chain is not the cheapest/fastest carrier.

### Future full 3-D implementation
A true cubical ECC could expose independent face/edge/vertex contact apertures in a monolithic or assembled 3-D cell fabric. This is FUTURE_PROCESS until physical process/yield is demonstrated.

## Architecture consequence
Local hierarchy becomes:
1. direct active cell contact;
2. short regenerative cell chain;
3. existing Nerve/subwire/event-spine for sparse/shared/long communication;
4. promoted electrical/Myelin chord where stable;
5. optional optical route only after real break-even.

The cells do not replace Charge Arteries or Thermal Capillaries; those remain separate physical service anatomy because mixing information, energy collection and heat removal into one contact would recreate disturbance and reliability problems.

## Acceptance status
KEEP at model level:
- cells as regenerative local communication fabric;
- selectable face/edge/vertex candidate contacts;
- face contacts as default;
- population confidence rather than one-cell uncertainty;
- quorum regeneration for noisy local paths;
- uncertainty causes continued local settling, not automatic computer fallback.

NOT YET PHYSICALLY CLOSED:
- capacitance/area of an ECC contact aperture;
- simultaneous multi-contact disturbance into weak Grammar evidence;
- PVT/mismatch of the local quorum/confidence circuit;
- realistic 3-D edge/vertex manufacturing;
- whole-region energy versus existing direct fourth-face + Nerve hierarchy.
