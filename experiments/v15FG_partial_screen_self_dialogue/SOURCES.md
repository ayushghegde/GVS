# External feasibility checks

These are used only to check physical scale; no published circuit/layout is copied into GVS.

- 2025 Optics Letters, "Micro-LED for short distance optical links with low power consumption": >1 GHz microLED bandwidth and 7.34 pJ/bit at a demonstrated operating point.
- 2026 Nature Communications, transfer-printed InGaN microLEDs: 20-um device demonstrated 1.5 Gbps at 25 uA and reported 0.056 pJ/bit transmitter-side energy.
- Intel optical I/O chiplet demonstration: ~5 pJ/bit class integrated optical I/O at very high bandwidth.

v15FG intentionally uses a much slower >=1-us observation window and therefore explores a lower-current dot regime; that exact operating point still requires a physical coupon.
