# v13A5 Physical Closure — Co-placed 10-MIM Ratio + 10-MOS Dual-Pair Reader

**Verdict: PASS for the present physical closure battery.**

This closes the original v13A5 readout problem using the later v13A6 dual-input-pair solution rather than the asymmetric 13-MOS analog swap reader.

## Why the reader changed

The original 13-MOS reader physically swapped weak `GC/GR` evidence through four NFET switches. Full layout showed a large side preference. Older Glyph work had already established the safer principle: do not force a fragile powered reader onto weak analog state when the information can stay local; if a robust comparison is required, keep the two physical orientations symmetric and only cross into robust/full-swing state after the analog decision.

The selected reader therefore uses two mirrored PFET evidence-input pairs around one regenerative core. Phase 0 senses candidate/reference normally; after reset and fresh motif replay, phase 1 uses the mirrored pair. The weak evidence never passes through an analog crossbar.

## Physical block

The final co-placed layout contains:

- 10 legal 2x2 um MIM capacitors for the candidate/reference ratio;
- 10 MOS in the dual-input-pair self-check reader;
- actual short physical `GC` and `GR` routes between array and reader;
- full extracted parasitic capacitance/resistance.

The combined layout is 0-DRC and extracts the intended 10 MIM + 10 MOS topology. Direct extracted `GC-GR` coupling is about 0.135 fF.

## Timing correction

At 3.5 ns/phase the co-placed block retained the correct evidence polarity but did not always regenerate above the 0.9 V robust threshold. The problem was local regeneration time, not a wrong-side offset.

A stronger tail control worked, but cost essentially the same energy. The cleaner choice is to retain the existing 0.70 V phase-tail control and allow **6 ns/phase**. At FF this gives materially more margin than 5 ns and avoids a stronger analog control requirement.

## Nominal PVT at 6 ns/phase

All six exact/partial TT/FF/SS cases pass.

- TT exact evidence: +22.12 / +21.78 mV; winners 1.170 / 1.152 V.
- TT partial: -21.52 / -21.24 mV; winners 1.242 / 1.229 V.
- FF exact: +22.34 / +21.84 mV; winners 1.114 / 1.104 V.
- FF partial: -21.70 / -21.27 mV; winners 1.199 / 1.189 V.
- SS exact: +21.84 / +21.61 mV; winners 1.254 / 1.229 V.
- SS partial: -21.28 / -21.09 mV; winners 1.308 / 1.300 V.

## Actual co-placed combined mismatch screen

The final extracted geometry was run with `tt_mm`, `ff_mm`, and `ss_mm`, with independent MIM and MOS mismatch active through the supplied SKY130 model library. Two launches per exact/partial corner were completed (seeds 101 and 404): **12/12 correct, 0 fallback, 0 wrong accept**.

The smallest absolute evidence margin observed was about **18.52 mV** (FF partial seed 101). Every tested phase still regenerated the correct physical side above 0.9 V while the losing side remained below 0.5 V.

This is a small mismatch screen, not a fabrication-yield Monte Carlo.

## Energy at selected 6 ns timing

Total absolute source-work proxy, including reader VDD and event/reset/tail sources:

- FF exact: ~75.87 fJ
- FF partial: ~79.71 fJ
- TT exact: ~83.08 fJ
- TT partial: ~86.35 fJ
- SS exact: ~93.88 fJ
- SS partial: ~96.53 fJ

Reader VDD alone is ~71.7-93.1 fJ across these six cases.

The energy is higher than the earlier schematic 33-64 fJ estimate because this is the actual co-placed extracted block and uses the safer 6 ns local evaluation window.

## Decision

v13A5 is physically closed for the current acceptance battery:

`physical 10-MIM PEX -> physical dual-pair reader PEX -> fresh motif replay -> two-phase mirrored self-check -> TT/FF/SS -> combined MIM+MOS mismatch -> energy`

Do not return to the 13-MOS analog swap reader. Do not replace the Grammar ratio with a fixed digital threshold.

Further compaction is allowed only if it materially improves area/energy without reducing the current physical margin or mismatch behavior.
