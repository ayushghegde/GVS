# v14M Evidence Sources

These sources support separate pieces of the v14M engineering envelope. They do **not** prove that one fabricated BDJ already meets all v14M targets simultaneously.

1. Chekol et al., Ag/HfO2/Pt diffusive memristor SET kinetics: volatile threshold switching around 0.2–0.3 V at 100 nA compliance and pulsed SET-kinetics measurements. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10060002/
2. Abbas et al., Nanoscale 2020: coexistence of threshold switching and nonvolatile memory switching in ALD HfO2 memristors by changing current conditions. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32597451/
3. Passerini et al., Scientific Reports 2025: one AgSn/SiO2/Pt alloyed memristor experimentally operated as volatile integrate/fire neuron and nonvolatile synaptic weight device by changing compliance/current regime. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-15251-x
4. Jeon et al., Nanoscale Horizons 2024: Ag/Ta2O5/HfO2/Pt diffusion memristor with controlled volatile threshold switching and short-/long-term synaptic behavior. https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2024/nh/d3nh00571b
5. Huang et al., Rare Metals 2026 review: thin-Ag Ag/HfOx filamentary devices summarized with sub-100-ns volatile switching, ultralow OFF current, and high nonlinearity. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/rar2.70514
6. Ag/HfO2/MoS2/Pt nonvolatile RF-switch work (2026): separate evidence that HfO2-family filament dynamics can reach ~55/70 ns nonvolatile switching in an optimized heterointerface. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chip.2026.100199
7. Sun et al., Nature Materials 2026: amorphous selenium OTS selector with ~20 ns switching, 4e-12 A leakage, >1e8 on/off ratio, and 2e9-cycle endurance. This is a speed/selector reference, not the selected v14M memory material. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41563-026-02499-5
8. Wang et al., Nano Energy 2025: HfO2-based ferroelectric memcapacitor with programmable capacitance, >1e9 endurance, >10-year retention claim/model, and 31 fJ/inference in its demonstrated neuromorphic system. This remains an alternative if diffusive-junction nonvolatile writes are too costly. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nanoen.2025.111011

## Evidence rule
Never combine the best voltage from one paper, best speed from another, and best endurance from a third and describe the combination as a measured v14M device. v14M uses these as independent feasibility constraints and target ranges only.
