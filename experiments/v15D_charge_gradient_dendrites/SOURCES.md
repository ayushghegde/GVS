# Sources / parameter anchors

External papers are used only to check constituent physics; no published chip/circuit/layout is copied.

- 4-nm HZO low-voltage/endurance anchor: ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces, 2022, DOI 10.1021/acsami.2c15369. Reports 0.6 V one-shot, 1.2 V stable-memory operation, >10-year retention, and 1e12 endurance for an optimized 4-nm HZO capacitor.
- 4-nm HZO polarization/coercive-voltage anchor: ACS Applied Electronic Materials, 2024, DOI 10.1021/acsaelm.4c01216. Reports ~0.8 V double coercive voltage and ~25 µC/cm² double remanent polarization at ±1 V.
- Volatile Ag ion relaxation sanity check: npj 2D Materials and Applications, 2025, `Threshold switching in vertically aligned MoS2/SiOx heterostructures based on silver ion migration`; reports spontaneous relaxation on the hundreds-of-ns scale in that device.

GVS-specific geometry, dual-time memory rule, need-based replay, charge-gradient selection, repair-branch behavior, and system integration are generated inside this experiment chain.
