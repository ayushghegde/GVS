# v14V external physics anchors

These sources are **not architecture sources**. v14V's self-addressed topology/control sequence was synthesized in GVS. The papers are used only to check whether isolated constituent phenomena have experimentally existed at useful scales.

1. Toprasertpong et al., *Low Operating Voltage, Improved Breakdown Tolerance, and High Endurance in Hf0.5Zr0.5O2 Ferroelectric Capacitors Achieved by Thickness Scaling Down to 4 nm for Embedded Ferroelectric Memory*, ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces 14 (2022), DOI: `10.1021/acsami.2c15369`. Used only for the 4-nm HZO voltage/retention/endurance scale.
2. *Threshold Switching of Ag-Ga2Te3 Selector with High Endurance for Applications to Cross-Point Arrays*, Nano Express 16, 128 (2021). Used only as evidence that passive volatile Ag-containing two-terminal structures can show very high ON/OFF selectivity and very low OFF current.
3. *Threshold switching in vertically aligned MoS2/SiOx heterostructures based on silver ion migration*, npj 2D Materials and Applications (2025). Used only as evidence that volatile Ag-ion switching can relax on a hundreds-of-nanoseconds timescale.

No text, layout, complete stack, or published circuit is copied into the v14V design.
